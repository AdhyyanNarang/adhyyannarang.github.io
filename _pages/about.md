My research spans reinforcement learning (RL), LLM alignment, and robust machine learning. I study how to train models that learn efficiently from feedback and remain reliable when that feedback is imperfect. My work combines theoretical foundations with empirical LLM research and industry experience applying ML to robotics, conversational AI, and personalization.

I recently completed my PhD in the [ECE Department](https://www.ece.uw.edu/) at the University of Washington, advised by [Prof. Maryam Fazel](https://faculty.washington.edu/mfazel/) and [Prof. Lillian Ratliff](http://faculty.washington.edu/ratliffl/about/). During my PhD, I also collaborated closely with Prof. Jeff Bilmes and Prof. Kevin Jamieson. **I am on the industry job market.**

Prior to joining UW, I completed my master's degree in 2020 in the [BLISS Lab](http://bliss.eecs.berkeley.edu/) in the [EECS Department](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/) at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), where I was advised by Prof. Anant Sahai. I completed my undergraduate studies at UC Berkeley with a major in EECS and a minor in Theater, Dance & Performance Studies.
