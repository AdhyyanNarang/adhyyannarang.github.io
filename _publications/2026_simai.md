SimAI models interactions among psychological factors to simulate user behavior. It supports controlled evaluations of prolonged AI conversations, including safety failures in interactions with vulnerable users.

Cheng Li, Adhyyan Narang, Jiayi Yuan, John F. Buss, Jina Suh, Devon Sandel-Fernandez, Yulia Tsvetkov, Adam M. Kuczynski, Tim Althoff.
